Overview

Runwal Raaya.

Inspired by the ancient Sanskrit word Raay. Raaya carries the quiet weight of royalty— a way of life that is poised, powerful, and innately confident.

Set across 4 acres in the heart of Worli, Raaya is a rare sanctuary with just two iconic towers.

Each residence is a realm of uncompromised luxury— private elevators, sunlit terraces, and amenities that whisper elegance.

Much like the promise it holds, Runwal Raaya delivers not just a home, but a world where life doesn’t adjust.

It dazzles.
